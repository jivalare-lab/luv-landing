# LUV landing

Single-page recruitment site for Loyola University Ventures, based on the November 16, 2026 concept packet from Jey Miguez. The source of the page is HTML, CSS, and a small JavaScript file. The supplied PNG was used as a visual reference, not as the page itself.

## Current scope

The page explains LUV and its Connect, Collab, Create invitation. The longer sections explain a way to approach building and point to New Orleans as context; they do not present hypothetical projects as current LUV work. The Loyola resource links point to the university's separate [Center for Entrepreneurship and Community Development](https://startup.loyno.edu/), [Savoie Ideation Lab](https://startup.loyno.edu/about/dr-bobby-lori-kent-savoie-ideation-lab), and [Wolf Pack LaunchU](https://startup.loyno.edu/programs/wolf-pack-launchu). They are labeled as separate from LUV.

The campus illustration is an original SVG based on [Holy Name of Jesus Church](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_Orleans_(LA,_USA)_Holy_Name_of_Jesus_Church.jpg) and an [official Marquette Hall photograph](https://css.loyno.edu/sites/default/files/2022-05/marquette.jpg). It is an illustration, not a surveyed skyline or an official university mark. Event-update signup is intentionally not active because the packet does not provide a mailing list, approved form destination, data owner, or privacy language. No visitor email is collected or stored.

The hero uses a photograph of a [St. Charles Avenue streetcar in front of Loyola](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:St_Charles_Avenue_Streetcar_in_front_of_Loyola_University_New_Orleans_Oct_2025.jpg) by Infrogmation, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The city section uses [musicians at Maison on Frenchmen Street](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frenchmen_Street,_New_Orleans,_November_2025_-_Band_at_Maison.jpg) by Jeremy Thompson, licensed CC BY 4.0. Both are stored locally as WebP and credited in the footer. The photographs do not depict LUV events or members.

An unmodified institutional logo has been prepared on the local `feat/loyola-official-logo` branch. It is awaiting confirmation of university marketing approval before public distribution. See [Loyola's logo rules](https://omc.loyno.edu/policies-procedures/loyola-brand-assets-logos-mascots-marks) and [approval requirements](https://omc.loyno.edu/policies-procedures/omc-approval-requirements).

## Local check

Run `node verificar.js` and open `index.html` in a browser.

## Before activating signup

The club should identify the list owner and platform, confirm the consent and unsubscribe wording, and test submission, storage, confirmation, and removal end to end. Signup for updates must remain distinct from formal club membership.
